Tech Leveler

Your faction's tech level goes up as you finish key research, one tier at a time. Research above your tier costs more, all the way up the ladder, including the tiers other mods add past Ultra.

Steam Workshop Source on GitHub

Tiers

Every tier has a list of key research. Finish all of it and your faction moves up to the next tier, and a letter tells you so. If you already have the key research for several tiers, you climb all of them at once. Your tier never goes down.

Your faction starts on the tier that matches its normal tech level. Vanilla code still sees one of its own tech levels, so each tier maps to one of them.

A tier with no key research can't be left. That is how the ladder ends, and it is also why Ultra stops your progress if none of the mods that add Ultra research are loaded.

Research cost

A project costs an extra 50% of its base cost for every tier it sits above your faction. Vanilla uses the same 50% step but stops counting at Industrial, so Spacer and Ultra research never cost more than Industrial research. Tech Leveler keeps counting up the whole ladder.

IndustrialSpacer×1.5×1.0

Research of your own tier or below costs its base price, unless you are proto in that tier (see below). The research window note shows both levels and the cost, for example: Your faction is industrial-level, the research is spacer-level. Base cost 1200 becomes 1800.

Other mods' tiers change the distances. With Beyond Our Reach loaded there are more steps between Spacer and Archotech, so jumping ahead costs more.

Proto tiers

A tier you just reached doesn't feel like home yet. You are proto in it, and research of that tier costs up to 25% extra.

  1. When you reach a tier, the letter tells you how much extra its research costs and how many of its key research projects you need to finish: 20% of them, rounded up.
  2. Each one you finish lowers the extra cost a step.
  3. Once you finish enough, a second letter tells you your colony is properly in the tier, and its research costs the normal price.

Example: you reach Spacer and it has 10 key research projects, so you need 2. Right after you arrive, Spacer projects cost ×1.25 (they were ×1.5 while you were Industrial). After one key research they cost ×1.125, after two ×1.0. The tier you start the game on is never proto.

The Tech tiers tab

Every research bench, including modded ones, has a Tech tiers tab. It lists every tier and marks which ones you have reached. Select one to see its key research: finished projects in green, missing ones in red with their progress. If a tier can't be left, or is the last one, the tab says why.

Click a project name to open it in the research window. This works with the vanilla window, Research Tree, Nice Research Tab and YART. If the project can't be shown there, its info card opens instead.

Compatibility

  • Beyond Our Reach :: Redux: adds Solar before Ultra, Quasar after it, and Tenebral and Umbral in the archotech range.
  • Armors For All Tech Levels, Guns For All Tech Levels, Beyond Our Reach: add the Advanced Spacer tier after Spacer.
  • Other mods: key research from Royalty, Biotech, Combat Extended, Dubs Bad Hygiene, Dead Man's Switch, Vanilla Vehicles Expanded, Rimputers, Rim-Effect and a few others is added to the matching tier when they are loaded.
  • YART: its era bands, colours and labels follow the Tech Leveler tiers, including the ones other mods add.
  • [LFS] UI Rethemed - Core: when active, the Tech tiers tab is drawn in its theme.
  • Not compatible: Tech Advancing by GHXX, which inspired this mod, and Vanilla Factions Expanded - Tribals. Both change the player faction's tech level and research cost too.
  • Requires Harmony.

For modders

Tiers, key research and tier tags are plain XML, so you can change them with ordinary patches. Research is referenced by defName and resolved at startup. A name that doesn't exist logs one warning and is skipped.

Add key research to a tier
Add <li> entries to the tier's keyResearch list, for example /Defs/TechLeveler.TechTierDef[defName="TL_Ultra"]/keyResearch. Use MayRequire so the entry is skipped when your mod isn't loaded. If the tier has no keyResearch list yet, add an empty one first.
Put a project in a tier
Add it to the tier's tierResearch list, or give the research def a TechLeveler.ResearchTierExtension. Otherwise a project goes in the lowest tier that matches its vanilla tech level.
Add a new tier
Add a TechLeveler.TechTierDef with an order (Spacer is 50, Ultra 60, Archotech 70) and a vanillaLevel. Wrap it in PatchOperationFindMod so it only exists with your mod.

Common/Patches/_Example_AddResearch.xml.example in the mod folder is a patch you can copy, and Common/Patches/BorRedux_Tiers.xml shows a full set of new tiers.
